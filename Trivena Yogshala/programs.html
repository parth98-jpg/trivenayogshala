FIND YOUR PRACTICE

Something For Everyone

At Trivena Yogshala, we bring together movement, mindfulness, creativity and healing through programs designed for different ages, interests and lifestyles.

01. Yoga

Adults

Whether you are a total beginner, navigating physical limitations, or looking to deepen your self-care, Our Yoga Session provides space where one can cultivate balance, strength, and explore inner peace at his/her own pace.

02. Zumba

Move With Rhythm

Zumba is a vibrant, high-energy cardio dance workout that turns fitness into an exhilarating celebration. It provides a full-body workout that burns calories, boosts endurance, and improves cardiovascular health. The upbeat atmosphere makes exercise feel effortless and fun, encouraging you to let loose, release stress, and elevate your mood. Suitable for all fitness levels, Zumba at Trivena Yogshala is the perfect way to dance your way toward better health and overflowing energy.

03. Kids Dance

Fun. Creative. Expressive.

Kids Dance provides a creative way to express themselves, develop rhythm, and build physical agility. Trivena Yogshala provides an inspiring space where kids can unleash their creative energy, make new friends, and discover the pure delight of movement.

04. Kids Yoga

Growing With Awareness

Kids Yoga at Trivena Yogshala offers a playful space where children can explore movement, build body awareness,and learn valuable mindfulness techniques in a fun, supportive environment. Yoga practice integrated with fun activites like painting, mat pilates, zumba would help child learn yoga and mindful activities in a creative environment. Focusing on Mantra chanting, yoga practices and mindful activities Kids are expected to accustomed with self discipline and integrety. It is a warm, nurturing space designed to help young minds and bodies grow with joy, balance, and self-expression.

05. Mat Pilates

Strength & Stability

Mat Pilates is a low-impact, highly focused discipline designed to strengthen the body's deep core muscles, improve posture, and enhance total body control. Utilizing body weight and deliberate, controlled movements, each session targets alignment, muscular endurance, and flexibility around the spine and joints. Mat Pilates helps prevent injuries, improves functional strength for daily activities, and tones muscles without adding bulk. It is an ideal practice for anyone looking to build a resilient core, refine their physical alignment, and achieve a balanced, graceful posture.

06. Awareness Programs

Pause. Reflect. Understand.

Our Awareness Programs are transformative workshops and sessions dedicated to mental wellness, self-discovery, and conscious living. Led by experienced facilitators, these programs explore topics such as stress management, emotional intelligence, mindfulness practices, and healthy lifestyle habits. Participants are equipped with actionable tools and practical guidance to navigate life's challenges with greater clarity, emotional resilience, and personal alignment. By fostering a deeper understanding of oneself and one's environment, these sessions help build a happier, more intentional life.

07. Sound Healing

Relax Through Sound

Sound Healing is a deeply restorative therapy that uses the therapeutic vibrations of instruments such as crystal singing bowls, gongs, and chime bells to restore harmony to your energy field. As you lie comfortably in a quiet space, the soothing frequencies wash over you, lowering heart rates, easing muscle tension, and calming an overactive mind. The acoustic vibrations work at a cellular level to release stored stress and promote deep brainwave states associated with meditation and physical healing. It is an effortless journey into relaxation, leaving you profoundly refreshed and aligned.

08. Reiki Healing

A Space For Relaxation

Reiki Healing is a gentle, non-invasive subtle energy practice that supports your body’s natural ability to heal, rebalance, and release blockages. Through light touch or hovering hands, our skilled practitioner channels universal life force energy into key energy centers across your body. This soothing process helps clear emotional distress, alleviate physical discomfort, and calm the nervous system. Whether you are dealing with burnout, emotional turbulence, or fatigue, a Reiki session offers a peaceful sanctuary to restore your subtle energy flow and nourish your inner peace.

09. Tarot Card Reading

Reflection & Personal Insight

Tarot Card Reading is an intuitive tool for self-reflection, clarity, and guidance on your personal life journey. Rather than predicting a fixed future, a Tarot session offers a fresh perspective on your current circumstances, emotional states, relationships, and hidden opportunities. Through symbolic imagery and guided dialogue, our reader helps illuminate underlying patterns and choices, empowering you to make confident decisions aligned with your highest good. It is an enlightening experience designed to bring clarity, validation, and insight when navigating life's transitions.
