WHO WE ARE

More Than Just Yoga

Welcome to Trivena Yogshala, our space serves as a harmonious confluence where mind, body, and spirit align. Grounded in authentic yogic traditions, Trivena Yogshala offers a welcoming avenue for practitioners of all levels—whether you are stepping onto the mat for the very first time or seeking to deepen a long-standing practice. Here, expert instructors guide you through purposeful movement, restorative breathwork, and deep meditation, empowering you to cultivate balance, strength, and clarity from the inside out.

At Trivena Yogshala, we believe that wellness knows no age, which is why we offer an all-inclusive suite of programs tailored for every generation. Our diverse schedule features dedicated Kids Yoga and Adults Yoga, high-energy Zumba, creative Kids Dance classes, and core-strengthening Mat Pilates. Beyond physical fitness, we nurture deep emotional and energetic well-being through life-enriching awareness programs, immersive Sound Healing sessions, gentle Reiki Healing, and insightful Tarot Card Readings. Whether you are looking to build physical strength, foster confidence in your children, or seek spiritual guidance, Trivena Yogshala provides the perfect sanctuary to align your mind, body, and soul.

Main Objective

To deliver comprehensive wellness services through yoga, pranayama, guided meditation, mindfulness practices, psychosocial support, and spiritual awareness programs.

To conduct trainings, workshops, and wellness retreats aimed at fostering social, emotional, and psychological well-being across diverse communities.

To offer accredited teacher training programs in yoga, sound healing, and integrative wellness practices, developing skilled human resource in the wellness sector.
